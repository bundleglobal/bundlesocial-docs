<Warning>
🥺 YOU CAN POST DIFFERENT CONTENT TO ALL THE PLATFORMS ALL AT ONCE 🥴
</Warning>

In this tutorial you will create, customize, and publish your first post across several platforms at once.

## Open the post creator

On the sidebar, click **Posts**.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/sidebar-posts.png' />
</Frame>

This is your **Posts** dashboard. It's empty for now - click the **+** icon to open the post creator.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/posts-dashboard-empty.png' />
</Frame>

The first page of the post creator is strictly internal. Here you pick which account to post on, what content to post, and when.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/post-creator.png' />
</Frame>

<Note>
  You can change all of this later, so if your creative juices aren't flowing
  from the get-go, don't worry.
</Note>

## Pick the platforms

At the top of the screen you'll see every account connected **to the team you're currently on**. Click a tile to select the account you want to post on.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/select-account.png' />
</Frame>

For this tutorial we'll post to all four connected platforms:

- **YouTube** and **Instagram** for video
- **LinkedIn** for company updates
- **Discord** to engage with the community

## Pick the content

You might be on the go and not have the right content on hand. That's why we have the **media library** - internal storage for all of your assets. [Learn more about it here.](/knowledge/posts/media-library)

Click **Select media**.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/select-media-button.png' />
</Frame>

Here you can see all of your media. Pick from what's there or **upload something new**. We'll add one new photo and pull the rest from the [media library](/knowledge/posts/media-library).

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/media-library.png' />
</Frame>

## Set the time

Say your boss wants a post every 3 hours. Instead of sitting down every 3 hours, pick a time once and forget about it.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/set-time.png' />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  If you don't set a time, or set one earlier than the current time, the post
  publishes as soon as processing finishes.
</Tip>

## Customize your content

In the bottom right corner, click **Customize**.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/customize-button.png' />
</Frame>

Your screen should now look like this:

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/customize-view.png' />
</Frame>

Starting from the top:

- **Title** - internal only
- **Date** - when to post

Below that are platform icons - these act as tabs. Click between them and you'll notice the fields change: YouTube has a **Text** field and a **Description** field, while Instagram only has **Text**.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/platform-tabs.png' />
</Frame>

### Adjust the content per platform

Each tab has its own content field. Hover over a piece of content to delete it - for example, on the YouTube tab we keep only the video we want to post.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/adjust-content-youtube.png' />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  To post multiple images - for example a carousel on Instagram - just leave the
  content untouched. We support multi-content on every platform that offers it.
</Warning>

Next, switch to the Instagram tab and remove any content you don't want there.

<Info>You can also add content at this step.</Info>

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/adjust-content-instagram.png' />
</Frame>

Now do the same for the remaining platforms.

## Discord and Slack quirk

If you post to Discord or Slack without changing the sender, your message shows up like this:

<Card>
  <Frame type='glass'>
    <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/discord-default-sender.png' />
  </Frame>
</Card>

That's fine if you don't mind. If you do, click the **+** icon to create a new bot.

<Card>
  <Frame type='glass'>
    <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/create-bot-button.png' />
  </Frame>
</Card>

A popup appears - upload a bot cover photo and give it a name. The name is shown as the sender in Discord/Slack, so choose carefully. Then click **Create**.

<Card>
  <Frame type='glass'>
    <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/create-bot-modal.png' />
  </Frame>
</Card>

Your bot is ready to reuse next time. **Don't forget to select the newly created bot from the dropdown.**

<Card>
  <Frame type='glass'>
    <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/select-bot.png' />
  </Frame>
</Card>

Now your Discord sender looks like this:

<Card>
  <Frame type='glass'>
    <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/discord-bot-sender.png' />
  </Frame>
</Card>

<Note>
  To learn more about bots, [read the bots guide](/knowledge/posts/dc-slack-bots).
</Note>

## Publish

With your captions and images in place, it's time to post. Click **Publish**.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/publish-button.png' />
</Frame>

The page refreshes and you're back on the **Posts** dashboard - this time with your post on it. If it includes a lot of content, like several large videos, the post may show a **processing** or **scheduled** status. Don't worry, the rest will be published.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/post-dashboard.png' />
</Frame>

You can also switch to the **calendar view** to see your scheduled and published posts laid out by date.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/posts/calendar-view.png' />
</Frame>
