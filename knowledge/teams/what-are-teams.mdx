In this tutorial you will learn how teams work in bundle.social - how to create them, switch between them, and manage their settings.

## What is a team?

Each organization can have many teams. A team is a **social set** - a group of social accounts that belong to a single entity, such as a company, a project, or a personal brand.

## Create a team

When you join for the first time, you'll be asked to create your first free team. You can upgrade it later.

## Link in bio

The **Link in bio** page is a single shareable page that collects all of your important links - perfect as the one URL in your social profiles.

Set an avatar, name, and description, add your social icons, and build a list of links. Each link can be toggled on or off, reordered by dragging, and has its own settings and click analytics. Use the **Preview** link at the top to see the public page, and click **Save** when you're done.

<Frame type='glass'>
  <img src='/images/knowledge/teams/link-in-bio.png' />
</Frame>

## Google Business

The **Google Business** page manages the Google Business profile connected to the current team. The banner at the top shows which location you're controlling.

Use the tabs to edit each part of the profile - Profile, Opening hours, Special hours, More hours, Categories, Attributes, Services, Food menus, and Place actions. The Profile tab covers the core public information: business name, website, phone, and description. Click **Save profile** to publish your changes.
