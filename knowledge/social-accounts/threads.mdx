In this tutorial, you will learn how to connect a Threads account to your team.

<h2>First things first, create a team or be a part of one</h2>
If you don't know what teams are, [click here](/knowledge/teams/what-are-teams)
to get up to speed.

## Connect your Threads account

Open **Social accounts** from the dashboard sidebar and click **Connect** on the Threads tile.

You will be redirected to the Threads authorization screen. Log in with the account you want to manage and allow the requested permissions.

After authorization, you will return to bundle.social. Threads does not require a separate channel selection step, so the account is ready once the connection succeeds.

## What you can do after connecting

- publish text posts,
- publish image and video posts,
- import replies,
- reply to imported replies,
- fetch profile and post analytics.

## Errors

<Warning>Error connecting Threads.</Warning>
This usually means the OAuth flow was interrupted, the account was not eligible, or Threads temporarily rejected the authorization. Try connecting again.

<Warning>You didn't grant all the necessary permissions.</Warning>
Reconnect and allow all requested permissions. Missing reply or insights permissions can block comment import or analytics later.
