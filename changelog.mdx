<Update label="2026-07-30">
Automatic first comment: a `firstComment` field on post create and update that publishes a comment as soon as the post itself goes live. Available for TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, LinkedIn, Reddit, Mastodon, Discord, Slack, and Bluesky, each with its own character limit. It is echoed back on create/update and single-post responses, and the underlying comment can still be edited through the comment endpoints.

AI content labels: `isAiGenerated` for X, Pinterest, and Instagram, applying each platform's native AI disclosure label.

Instagram paid partnerships: `isPaidPartnership` for the native "Paid partnership" label, and `brandedContentSponsors` to tag up to 2 sponsor usernames (accounts connected through Facebook Login only).

Snapchat videos now accept 5-180 seconds, up from 6-60.

`DELETE /misc/instagram/comment` to permanently delete a published Instagram comment, also available from the dashboard's post comments panel.

Dashboard translations for Danish, Thai, Korean, and Hebrew.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-20">
Threads post options: single `topicTag`, `replyControl`, `linkAttachment`, `poll`, `gif` (GIPHY), `allowlistedCountryCodes`, and Instagram Story cross-posting. Polls, GIFs, and link attachments are text-only.

YouTube monetization: revenue/monetization data in raw analytics when an account connects with `withBusinessScope` (the monetary analytics scope) and raw analytics are enabled for the organization. Channel-level and per-video revenue reports; channels not in YPP return `"no data available"`.

`withBusinessScope` now applies to YouTube in both the hosted and custom connect flows.

Twitter/X is moving to a usage-based billing model. X API posting costs are passed through at cost, 1:1, with no markup.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-12">
Snapchat Public Profile integration: OAuth connection, scheduled Stories and Spotlights, normalized profile and content analytics, and CSV bulk-post support.

Post history import improvements across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, and Threads, including more consistent imported media metadata.

Instagram business discovery can now return recent public media items.

Experimental `tiktokForceLogin` option for hosted and custom OAuth flows when TikTok keeps reusing the wrong active account.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-06-23">

Video uploads increased to 5 GB through the large upload flow. Upload-from-URL handling was also made more reliable.
</Update>
